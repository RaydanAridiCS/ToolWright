# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Toolwright to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Credit others' contributions.
- Respect people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused, and say so when you can no longer work on something you claimed.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments; sexualized language or imagery; personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, doxxing, or publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately misleading contributions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report concerns privately by emailing the maintainers at the address listed in the repository's profile, or by using GitHub's private reporting if enabled. All reports are handled confidentially and fairly.
